# Format bank: one idea, six formats

Made-up example, written for this skill.

**Brief:** a bike mechanic. Video shows a squeaky chain, then cleaning it with a rag and a cheap degreaser in 3 minutes, then a silent ride. Facts: has fixed bikes for 12 years.

**Contrast:** viewers think a squeaky chain needs more oil. The video shows it needs cleaning first, then less oil.

| Format | Spoken | On-screen text | First shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fortune Teller | "Next time your chain squeaks, you'll know why oil makes it worse." | Oil makes it worse | Close-up of the black, gunked chain |
| Experimenter | "I cleaned this chain for 3 minutes instead of oiling it. Listen." | 3 min, no new oil | Pedal turning, squeak audible |
| Teacher | "How to stop a squeaky chain in 3 minutes without a bike shop." | Silent chain in 3 min | Rag on the chain |
| Investigator | "Why your chain still squeaks after you oil it." | It's not dry | Oil dripping onto a dirty chain |
| Contrarian | "Stop oiling a squeaky chain. Clean it first." | Stop oiling it | Hand pulling away the oil bottle |
| Magician (layer) | Split screen: black chain and clean chain, squeak then silence | Same chain, 3 min apart | Before and after side by side |

**Top pick:** Investigator over the Magician split. Clear subject, a true belief to flip, and the picture proves it in the first second.
**Dropped:** "12 years of fixing bikes taught me this." Proof that's about the creator, not the viewer. Use it as a line later, not as the hook.
