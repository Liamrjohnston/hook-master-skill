# Credits

Hook Master is written from scratch in our own words. No sentences were copied from any creator, book or skill below.

## Creators

| Source | What we learned |
|---|---|
| Kallaway (YouTube: "Give me 15 mins, and I'll make your hooks impossible to skip"; "I Studied 100 Viral Hooks, These 6 Will Make You Go Viral") | The hook's two jobs, the four ways it fails (delay, confusion, irrelevance, disinterest), the six hook formats, building from the key visual, and making the words, picture, text and sound agree |
| Mino Lee (YouTube: "how i script videos to get 1M+ views every time"; "how i create viral hooks with ai that get 1M+ views every time") | Opening on a pain the viewer already has, concrete detail, pairing a useful idea with a proven format, and studying winners against the creator's own normal |

Both creators' view counts and results are their own reports. We did not use their courses, tools or private databases.

## Books (through the authors' talks and own pages, not the full books)

| Source | What we learned | How we accessed it |
|---|---|---|
| Jonah Berger, "Contagious" | High-arousal emotion, social currency, and the retell test | Two talks by Berger (Talks at Google, Wharton) |
| Chip Heath and Dan Heath, "Made to Stick" | Unexpected then makes sense, concrete beats clever, the curse of knowledge | A Chip Heath keynote, Dan Heath interviews, their written Q&A and book page. Some points are from summaries and are marked second hand in our notes |
| Brendan Kane, "Hook Point" | Say what it's about within 3 seconds, earn the next 5, something moves in the first second, compare with your own flops | Kane's own videos and a podcast interview. His figures are self-reported |
| George Loewenstein (1994), "The psychology of curiosity" | Curiosity needs a gap the viewer can see, and it fades fast | Abstract only, second hand |
